# Privacy Policy for Aleksi

*Last Updated:* September 15, 2026

Aleksi ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use the Aleksi Chrome extension (the "Extension").

## 1. Information We Collect

We value your privacy and aim to collect the absolute minimum amount of data required to maintain the Extension.

**Clicked Text Content:** The specific words or text segments that you explicitly click on while using the Extension are recorded and transmitted to our backend server.

**IP Addresses:** When the Extension makes network requests to our backend server, our server automatically logs your IP address. 

We do not collect personal identifiers (such as your name, email address, or billing info), general browsing history, keystrokes, or any data from the websites you visit outside of the text you intentionally interact with.

## 2. How We Use the Information

We use the logged IP addresses for a single, narrow purpose:

**Core Extension Functionality:** Recorded words are processed by our server to deliver the core features of the Extension (e.g., text analysis, translation, lookup, or data routing).

**Unique User Analytics:** We process IP addresses solely to count the number of active, unique users interacting with our service. This helps us understand system load and overall user engagement.

## 3. Data Retention and Security

No Permanent Storage: IP addresses are processed dynamically or stored in anonymized server logs. We do not build individual user profiles or track your behavior over time.

Security: We use standard industry practices, including HTTPS encryption for all data transit, to ensure that the network requests made by the Extension are secure.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third-party advertisers, brokers, or external companies.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
